Questions

Good to know

Why tip the bottle neck-down?

A cork that dries out shrinks and lets air into the wine. With the neck a little low, the wine stays against the cork, the same reason cellars store bottles on their side.

How do two stack?

Flip one wedge over so its curve sits on the shelf, then set the second on it, ribs to ribs. Point the thick ends opposite ways for level, the same way for steeper.

How do I clean it?

Wipe it with a damp cloth or wash it by hand. Keep it out of the dishwasher and hot cars; PLA softens with heat.

Can I pick colors?

Yes. The body and the grip are separate colors, and the first colorways come with the first batch.
